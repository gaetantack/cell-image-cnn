# Random forest: which features matter (DRAFT — Claude, 2026-10-25, for you to check)

*Validation results — not the final test numbers. Source: `results/rf_importance_val.csv`, `figures/rf_importance.png` (made by `scripts/fig_rf_importance.py`).*

The random forest on 28 hand-crafted features (best of 12 configurations: `max_features` = 0.33, `min_samples_leaf` = 5, `class_weight` = balanced, 500 trees) reached a validation macro-F1 of 0.631 ± 0.001 (mean ± SD over seeds 0–2) on four classes, against 0.154 for the majority and 0.256 ± 0.006 for the stratified chance baseline. Permutation importance on the validation cells was dominated by cell size: shuffling the mask area lowered macro-F1 by 0.113 ± 0.014, three times more than any other feature, and the major and minor axis lengths and the perimeter were also among the top eight. This matches the expected biology: a cell roughly doubles its contents between G1 and G2, and in the training cells the median mask area rises from 480 px (G1) to 570 px (S) and 668 px (G2). The next most important features were brightfield texture (GLCM correlation, 0.037) and darkfield texture (GLCM energy, 0.022). A cautious reading is that these capture changes in internal structure (for example chromatin condensation, which alters how light is scattered) as cells approach mitosis: median brightfield GLCM correlation falls from 0.82 in G1 to 0.71 in metaphase. Because these are correlations measured on a bright-core mask, this interpretation needs support from images before it goes into the paper. Mitotic cells were the hardest class for the random forest (validation recall 0.26 at seed 0), even with balanced class weights. Telophase cells stood out only by shape (median eccentricity 0.82 versus 0.27–0.35 for the other phases), but they are too few to raise the merged mitosis recall.

**To check:** (1) the biological reading of the texture features; (2) whether to report per-phase medians from train (used here) or train+val; (3) the 0.26 mitosis recall: it is the seed-0 value of the best macro-F1 configuration; configurations with `min_samples_leaf` = 20 reached about 0.51 mitosis recall at a lower macro-F1.
